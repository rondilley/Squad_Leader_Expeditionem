# RV-1: Squad Leader (Avalon Hill, 1977) counter inventory and Quick Reference Data Card

Research date: 2026-09-07. Scope: the base game only. Cross of Iron, Crescendo of Doom, GI: Anvil of Victory, and Advanced Squad Leader are out of scope, but item 9 refers to them.

## How to read this document

- Each fact has a source tag in square brackets. The References section gives the URL for each tag.
- "As printed" means that I read the value from a 600 DPI scan of the counter sheets [S1]. The scan is of the 4th edition (1980 rules, 1990s printing), not the 1977 printing. No source says that the counter values changed between editions. The SQLA inventory [S2] and the Grognard manifest [S6] agree with the scan, and the two refer to the 1977 game.
- "Not found" means that no source that I could open showed the value.
- I could not open BoardGameGeek pages (HTTP 403 and a Cloudflare page), the VASSAL module page (client-rendered, no text), or Scribd document pages other than page 1 images. The WebSearch tool has a limit of 200 uses, and I used all of them.

### 1.3 Disagreements between sources

- German MkIVF2: the SQLA table says 4 [S2]. The scan shows 3 MkIVF2 and 2 MkIVF1 [S1]. The fan facsimile CSV also says 3 [S8].
- American leaders: SQLA says 20 with three 9-2 leaders [S2]. The scan shows 21: 13 named leaders, one blank leader, and 7 rank-only leaders printed on the back of "Broke" counters, and these include a fourth 9-2 (1st Lt) [S1]. The fan CSV also gives 21 American leader counters [S8].
- Sheet count: the fan facsimile readme says 250 counters on each 1/2" sheet [S8]. The scan and the Grognard manifest show 260 on each sheet [S1] [S6]. The CSV of the same fan project sums to 520 for the two sheets [S8].
- Bazooka identification: SQLA shows the "6 [4] X11+" counters as bazookas [S2]. The fan CSV names the same six counters "c_eq_us_baz" [S8]. The scan agrees: six counters [S1].

- German: light blue with black print. The fan facsimile uses SVG "skyblue" (#87CEEB) [S8].
- Russian: tan (light brown) with black print. The manifest says "brown/black" [S6]. The fan facsimile uses "goldenrod" (#DAA520) [S8].
- American: yellow-green (chartreuse) with black print. The manifest says "green/black" [S6]. The fan facsimile uses #D5D22F and says the 1977 color is "horrid" with "no good match" [S8].
- Russian berserk: red with black print, red back with no print [S1]. Wikipedia says "Especially popular are the red 'berserk' counters" [S3].
- Markers: white with black print. Prep Fire and SR have red or orange starbursts. FFE is orange. The SR back is light blue [S1].

This answers the "Axis" photo question: tan counters with 4-4-7 and 6-2-8 squads and red berserk counters are Russian. The German counters in the 1977 base game are light blue [S1] [S8].

Style [S1] [S15]:

- Counter dimension 1/2" for infantry, leaders, support weapons and markers, and 5/8" for vehicles, guns and terrain markers [S3] [S7].
- Squads: two black silhouettes of walking riflemen
- Elite squads: two running figures with submachine guns
- Crews: two smaller figures
- Leaders: one figure with the rank and name in small vertical text at the right
- Support weapons: a black side-view icon (tripod MG, mortar, backpack radio, tube for panzerfaust and bazooka, satchel for the demolition charge, nozzle for the flamethrower).
- Vehicles: a top-down black and white silhouette put diagonally, name in small text along the silhouette, values in bold sans-serif numerals.
- The typeface is a bold condensed sans-serif. No source names the 1977 font. The fan facsimile uses "Acumin Variable Concept" as an alternative [S8].
- The 5/8" counters were "prepared by Roger MacGowan" and "included values for the weaponry, movement, a name and a top down silhouette. A second level of information was presented with typographic conventions (circles, boxes, asterisks, etc.) and [a third] level was presented with color" [S15]. (Quoted text, one phrase shortened in brackets.)

## 9. The dark green counter set

Identification: Cross of Iron (Avalon Hill, 1978) Rumanian (Romanian) counters.

Evidence from the SQLA Cross of Iron inventory [S16], shown verbatim:

- "20 347(6) RUMANIAN SQUADS"
- "02 8-1(7) RUMANIAN INFANTRY LEADER"
- "03 8-0(7) RUMANIAN INFANTRY LEADER"
- "02 7-0(6) RUMANIAN INFANTRY LEADER"
- "02 6+1(5) RUMANIAN INFANTRY LEADER"
- "01 188-2(7) RUMANIAN SNIPER"
- "01 188-1(7) RUMANIAN SNIPER"
- "03 4-10 MMG RUMANIAN MEDIUM MACHINE GUN"
- "06 2-6 LMG RUMANIAN LIGHT MACHINE GUN"

Agreement with the photo description:

- 3-4-7 squads: agree.
- 6+1 and 8-0 leaders: agree.
- "MMG 4-10 B11+": agree on 4-10.
- "1-8-8": agree. The "188" is a sniper counter (1-8-8 with a -1 or -2 modifier). Cross of Iron also has "188" snipers for German, SS and Russian forces [S16].
- "LMG 2-7 B11+": the inventory says 2-6. Check the photo again. A "2-6" is possible.

The color: no text source that I could open gives the Rumanian counter color. The SQLA file gives no colors [S16]. The agreement of five printed values makes the identification strong. Confirm the color with a photo of the Cross of Iron countersheet.

Rejected candidates:

- GI: Anvil of Victory American counters have 3-4-7 half-squads and 1-8-8 snipers [S10]. But the file shows no 6+1 leader and no MMG 4-10, and the American MMG is 4-12 in the base game.
- Crescendo of Doom: I could not open an inventory text (the SQLA file returns 404).
- ASL Axis Minor counters are green [S17]. But ASL counters do not use the "B11+" notation, and the task limits candidates to the Squad Leader series.

## Summary of confidence

- High: totals (520 and 192), all printed values of squads, crews, leaders, support weapons and vehicles. Also marker types and counts, colors, Infantry Fire Table, Close Combat Table, Support Weapons Chart, and the item 9 identification.
- Medium: wreck "S" numbers, and the agreement between the 1977 and 4th edition counters (no source says the values changed).
- Not found: To Hit Table, AFV Kill Table, Hit Determination modifiers, vehicle movement costs, Immobilize numbers, Artillery Module Selection Table, Spreading Fire Table, full Terrain Effects table.
